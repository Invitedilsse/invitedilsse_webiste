App Features

Awesome Features

All Events, One App!

Manage all your digital and offline invitations seamlessly in one App.

Always Know Where to Be

WhatsApp doesn’t remind you about your events. we offer automatic, timely reminders to keep you on schedule.

Goodbye to WhatsApp Chaos

No more searching through chats. Your invites are saved, searchable, and accessible whenever you need them.

Premium Look & Feel

Transform your invites from simple images to immersive experiences. Include striking visuals, event videos, and messages.

Multiple Events, One Flow

One invite page for all your wedding events Grithpaan, Haldi, Bharat, etc with date, time, venue & map.

No more back-and-forth messages!

Chat In App

Guests can share invitations with family & coordinate event details, ensuring everyone is on the same page.

Organized, Not Scattered

Where WhatsApp mixes event details within conversations, this service provides a dedicated space for your invites.

Notes & Reminders

Guests can save personalized notes for each event, such as 'Buy gift' or 'Wear ethnic, etc' conveniently in the app
